# TLF35584

## Definition

`TLF35584` is an automotive multi-rail power supply and safety PMIC intended for microcontroller-based systems. Based on the currently ingested manual chapters, it integrates pre-regulation, multiple post regulators, voltage monitoring, state-machine based power sequencing, watchdogs, interrupt generation, and safe-state behavior.

## Why It Matters

- It is the core external power and supervision device for the target driver and detailed-design workflow.
- Its behavior is not limited to simple power enable or disable; driver logic must align with state transitions, wake behavior, monitoring, watchdog servicing, and interrupt handling.
- A useful driver design will likely need to treat the device as a coordinated stateful subsystem rather than as a flat SPI register block.

## Key Capabilities Seen In Current Sources

- Multi-voltage supply outputs, including communication, microcontroller, standby, reference, and tracker supplies
- State-machine controlled transitions across `INIT`, `NORMAL`, `WAKE`, `SLEEP`, `STANDBY`, and `FAILSAFE`
- Configurable window watchdog and functional watchdog
- Interrupt generation for non-severe but actionable events
- Monitoring of output rails and supervision-triggered reset or failsafe reactions
- Wake and enable inputs that can trigger exits from low-power states
