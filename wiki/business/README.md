# Business

Store business-layer knowledge pages here.

This area is for project-specific functional meaning, policy, and intent, not generic chip theory.

Use this folder for pages such as:

- wake-up strategy
- reset classification policy
- watchdog service policy
- safe-state policy
- fault handling and degradation policy
- power-domain usage policy
- business-level mode transitions
- startup-readiness policy
- upper-layer integration contract
- self-test and service-phase policy

Typical questions answered here:

- Why is this feature used this way in the project
- Which behavior is required by product logic
- Which actions are mandatory, optional, or forbidden
- Which rules are project policy rather than hardware capability

Keep these pages linked to related concept, project, and decision pages.
