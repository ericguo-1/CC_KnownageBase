# Wiki

This is the persistent knowledge layer maintained by Codex.

## Areas

- `business/`: business-layer rules, policy, and usage semantics
- `concepts/`: concepts, patterns, and methods
- `entities/`: people, tools, products, chips, organizations
- `projects/`: project-centric or module-centric pages
- `summaries/`: source summaries and cross-source syntheses
- `decisions/`: decisions, tradeoffs, and rationale

## Rule

Pages should be focused, linked, and durable. Avoid chat-like notes here.

Keep concept, implementation, business, and decision knowledge separated when possible.
